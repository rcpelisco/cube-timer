Privacy policy

Cube Timer · last updated 14 November 2026

Cube Timer is a speedcubing timer made by one person as a hobby project. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.

If you don't sign in

Everything you do stays in this browser on this device: your solves, sessions, algorithm progress, Trainer times, settings, and a timer background photo if you choose one. None of it is sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and only used to sync between your devices.

  • Your Google account: Firebase Authentication (by Google) keeps your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID, so you can sign in.
  • Your cubing data: your solves, sessions, algorithm progress and settings, and Trainer times are stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account. Security rules let only your account read or change them.
  • Appearance and Trainer settings and your timer background photo are never uploaded.

Google stores this data for the app and may keep it on servers outside your country. Google's own handling of it is described in the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Abuse protection

When the app talks to Firebase, Firebase App Check uses Google reCAPTCHA to make sure the requests come from this app and not from a script. reCAPTCHA collects information about your device and browser for that check, under the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Hosting

The site is served by Firebase Hosting. Like any web server, it receives your IP address and browser details when it sends you the page, and Google may keep these in short-lived logs.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept until you delete it. In Settings, under Account, Delete account removes your cubing data and your sign-in record from Firebase. Sign out and remove from this device also clears this browser. You can export your solves from Settings at any time, as a csTimer file or a spreadsheet.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. For questions or requests about your data, open an issue at github.com/rcpelisco/cube-timer/issues.
